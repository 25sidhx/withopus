  Core Content Type: The 10 Emotional Dimensions

  Each day's content weaves one or more of these emotional dimensions:

  1. Ritual — "I do this every morning. It is my anchor."
  2. Intention — "I chose this because I want to start right."
  3. Calm — "I feel at peace when I eat here."
  4. Discipline — "I wake up early for this. It is my vow."
  5. Nourishment — "This feeds my soul."
  6. Connection — "We come here every week. We are not alone."
  7. Freshness — "I start fresh with this."
  8. Stillness — "I take a breath here. Nothing else."
  9. Hope — "I know today will be good because I had this."
  10. Belonging — "You’re not alone here. We’ve all been waiting."

  Every video, every image, every caption exists to honor one of these.
