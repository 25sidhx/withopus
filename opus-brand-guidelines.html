01 — Foundation
Brand Overview
Mission

To help brands find their creative identity and express it through cinematic, intentional social media content — content that stops the scroll and stays in memory.

Vision

To become the go-to content studio for Indian brands and creators who refuse to look like everyone else.

Positioning

Opus is a premium social media content agency based in Nagpur. We are not a generic agency. We craft the visual identity and content strategy for brands that have something real to say.

Core Values

Craft over speed. Every post is considered.

Warmth over noise. We don't shout — we draw people in.

Minimal over cluttered. Less, but better.

Brand Personality

Premium

We work with brands that value quality. Our output reflects that — no templates, no shortcuts.

Cinematic

Everything we create has a film-like warmth and depth. Content that feels like it was directed.

Minimal

We believe in the power of empty space. What is NOT on the screen matters as much as what is.

Clear Space Rule

Minimum Clear Space

Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the lowercase "o". No text, graphics, or decorative elements should enter this zone.

Minimum size: 80px wide for digital. 20mm wide for print.

✓ Do
  • Use gradient version on dark backgrounds
  • Use white version on gradient/colored backgrounds
  • Maintain clear space at all times
  • Use only approved color versions
  • Keep letterforms intact — never stretch or skew
✗ Don't
  • Add drop shadows or outer glows to the wordmark
  • Place logo on busy, cluttered backgrounds
  • Change font, weight, or letter spacing
  • Use any other gradient not in the brand system
  • Outline or stroke the letterforms
  • Rotate or skew the wordmark

Typography Rules

✓ Do
  • Use light weight (300) for large display text
  • Keep headline copy short and punchy
  • Use generous line-height for body text (1.6–1.8)
  • Apply wide letter-spacing to uppercase labels
✗ Don't
  • Use bold (700+) weight for headings — too aggressive
  • Mix more than 2 fonts in a single design
  • Use all-caps for body paragraphs
  • Use script or decorative fonts anywhere
05 — Aesthetics
Visual Style
Core Visual Direction

Warm Cinematic Minimalism. Every Opus visual is built around three pillars: warmth (the amber-to-violet gradient), texture (grain overlay that gives organic depth), and space (intentional emptiness that makes content breathe).

Gradient Texture — The Signature Element

How to Apply

The grainy gradient bloom is Opus's most distinctive visual element. It appears in:

  • Hero/cover images — full bleed gradient with grain
  • Post backgrounds — subtle gradient bloom in corners
  • Reel intros/outros — gradient as motion element
  • Website hero section — large format gradient background

Grain intensity: 4–8% opacity noise overlay. Enough to feel organic, not enough to look degraded.

Photography & Image Style

Lighting

Warm, directional light sources. Golden hour aesthetic. Avoid cold/blue-tinted photography. Images should feel like they belong in the same warm universe as the brand palette.

Composition

Generous negative space. Subject often off-center. Dark backgrounds where possible. Avoid overcrowded, busy compositions — every visual element should earn its place.

Editing Style

Slightly desaturated except warm tones. Lifted blacks (not pure black). Warm shadows. Subtle film grain in photography to match brand texture system.

What to Avoid

Stock photo aesthetic. Overly bright, HDR images. Cold blue-white color grading. Busy flat-lay compositions. Anything that looks corporate or generic.

Icon Style

Icon Guidelines

Style: Thin line icons only. 1–1.5px stroke weight. Rounded line caps. No filled/solid icons.

Color: Cream #F5F0E8 on dark backgrounds. Amber #E8A034 for highlighted/active states.

Size: Minimum 20px. Preferred 24px for UI use.

Content Tone

For Opus Own Content

Provocative but minimal. Say one thing per post. Lead with insight, not promotion. Never explain the joke. Trust the audience to connect the dots.

For Client Content

Adapt to client's voice while maintaining Opus visual standards. Every client's content should look like it belongs in the same premium universe — even if the tone changes.

Reel Style

Motion Guidelines

Transitions: Slow dissolves, warm light wipes, gradient sweeps. Avoid hard cuts in brand content.

Pacing: Slow and deliberate. Let frames breathe. Match the minimal brand energy.

Text animation: Fade up from below. No bouncy, springy animations — too playful for the brand.

Color grading: Warm shadows, desaturated cool tones, match the amber-violet brand palette.

07 — Voice
Brand Voice
Opus sounds like

Confident. Direct. Thoughtful. A creative who knows their craft and doesn't need to prove it. Warm but not soft. Premium but not arrogant.

Opus never sounds like

A generic agency. Buzzword-heavy. Over-explaining. Loud or sales-y. Corporate. Trying too hard to be cool or relatable.

Voice Principles

Short over long

If it can be said in 5 words, don't use 10. Every word must earn its place. Captions, headlines, taglines — always trim ruthlessly.

Show, don't explain

Don't write "we create premium content." Show a piece of premium content. Don't say "we're different." Let the work prove it.

Confident, not arrogant

State things. Don't qualify, hedge, or add "we think" to every sentence. Own the perspective.
